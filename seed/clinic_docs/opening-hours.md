# Opening hours

## Daily hours

The clinic is open every day of the week.

- Morning session: 09:00 to 13:00
- Afternoon session: 14:00 to 19:00

The clinic is closed between 13:00 and 14:00 for the lunch break. All times are local clinic time.

## Appointments and walk-ins

Appointments are 30 minutes long and start on the hour or the half hour. The first appointment of the day starts at 09:00 and the last one starts at 18:30.

Walk-in patients are seen only if a slot is free. We recommend booking an appointment in advance.

## Front desk and phone

You can call the front desk during opening hours to book, change or cancel an appointment.

Outside opening hours the clinic cannot take calls. In an emergency, call the emergency number immediately.
