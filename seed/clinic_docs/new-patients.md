# New patients

## Registering with the clinic

You do not need to register before your first visit. Book an appointment in the chat or by phone, and the front desk registers you when you arrive.

## Registration form

At your first visit you fill in a short registration form with your full name, date of birth, phone number and a contact person we can call if needed. Front-desk staff can help you fill it in. Allow extra time for this before your appointment.

## Documents for registration

To register, show a valid photo ID (national ID card or passport) and your insurance card, if you are insured. Our visit preparation information lists what to bring to every visit.

## Your patient number

After registration you receive a patient number. Give it when you call the front desk, so staff can find your appointments quickly.

## Updating your details

If your phone number, address or insurance changes, tell the front desk at your next visit or by phone, so we can reach you about your appointments.
