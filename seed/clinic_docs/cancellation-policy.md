# Cancellation policy

## How to cancel

You can ask to cancel an appointment in the chat or by calling the front desk during opening hours. Every cancellation is reviewed by front-desk staff. Your appointment stays booked until staff approve the cancellation, and you will receive a message with the decision.

## Notice

Please cancel as early as possible so we can offer the slot to another patient.

## Same-day appointments

Appointments booked for the same day are held for you until front-desk staff approve them. You will receive a message when the booking is confirmed or if it cannot be accepted.

## Changing an appointment

To move an appointment to another time, cancel the current appointment and book a new free slot. The new booking is confirmed separately.
