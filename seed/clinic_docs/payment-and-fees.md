# Payment and fees

## How to pay

You pay at the front desk at the end of your visit. We accept cash and debit or credit cards. Payment cannot be made in the chat.

## Consultation fees

The fee depends on the doctor and the type of visit. The front desk can tell you the current consultation fee before you book, by phone or at the desk. The fee is the same whether you book in the chat or by phone.

## Insured patients

If your insurer has a direct billing agreement with us, the front desk sends the claim to your insurer. You pay only the co-payment or deductible that your policy requires. Our insurance information explains which insurers we work with and what happens if a claim is refused.

## Receipts and invoices

You receive a printed receipt after every payment. If you need an itemised invoice, for example for your employer or your insurer, ask the front desk and we will prepare it.

## Questions about a bill

If you think a bill is wrong, contact the front desk during opening hours and keep your receipt. Staff will go through the bill with you and correct any mistake.
